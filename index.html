Ultimate Tense Review ⚔️

Before we move forward, let's make sure we are experts at choosing between the Simple Past and the Present Perfect.

Simple Past

Finished Time

The action is completely finished, and we know exactly when it happened.

Keywords: yesterday, last week, in 2020, ago, when I was...

"I started high school in 2021."

Present Perfect

Unfinished Time / Experience

Life experiences (time doesn't matter) OR actions with a result right now.

Keywords: already, just, yet, ever, never, recently, so far

"I have already finished my homework."

Fix the Mistakes

These sentences have tense errors. Rewrite them correctly in your notebook. Think about the time markers!

1. I have went to the cinema yesterday.
2. Did you ever eat Thai food?
3. She didn't finish her project yet.
4. We have bought a new car in 2022.
5. Look! I just spilled coffee on my shirt!

1. I went to the cinema yesterday. (Simple Past for 'yesterday')

2. Have you ever eaten Thai food? (Present Perfect for 'ever' / life experience)

3. She hasn't finished her project yet. (Present Perfect for 'yet')

4. We bought a new car in 2022. (Simple Past for specific finished year)

5. Look! I have just spilled coffee on my shirt! (Present Perfect for 'just')

B. Write Your Answers

Use your notebook to draft your answers to these two classic interview questions. Use the templates if you need help!

Question 1: "Tell me about yourself."

Include who you are now, what experience you have, and your personality.

Template Help: "I am a [high school student / university student]. I haven't worked in a company yet, but I have [played on a sports team / volunteered / studied X]. I am very [adjective] and [adjective]."

Question 2: "Why should we hire you?"

Focus on your specific skills and how they help the company. You must sell yourself as the solution to their needs!

Template 1: For Entry Level/Students "You should hire me because I am a highly motivated and fast learner. While I am building my professional experience, I have a strong foundation in [subject/club/sport] and I have successfully [mention an achievement]. I have excellent [skill] skills."
Template 2: Focus on Personality "You should hire me because my skills match the job requirements perfectly. I have already developed my [soft skill, e.g. communication] and [soft skill, e.g. teamwork] through [mention school/volunteer experience]. I am very [adjective] and [adjective]."

C. The Mock Interview

Work in pairs. One person is the interviewer, the other is the applicant. Read your answers aloud and practice sounding confident!

The Interviewer's Job

  • Ask the questions clearly.
  • Grammar Check: Did they use the Present Perfect correctly? (Have + V3)
  • Content Check: Did they actually answer the question?
  • Ask one follow-up question. (e.g., "Oh, you volunteered? Where did you volunteer?")

The Applicant's Job

  • Speak loudly and confidently.
  • Look the interviewer in the eye.
  • Don't just read your paper—try to speak naturally!
  • Be ready to answer a surprise follow-up question using the Simple Past.
